### About My Research

My PhD research explores the intersection of **context-aware time series forecasting** and **Implicit Neural Representations (INRs)**. 

I work on designing models capable of leveraging complex exogenous dynamics, metadata, and environmental context to improve predictive accuracy, while investigating INRs to capture continuous-time signals and non-standard temporal resolutions.

### About My Research

My PhD research explores the intersection of **context-aware time series forecasting** and **Implicit Neural Representations (INRs)**. 

I work on designing models capable of leveraging complex exogenous dynamics, metadata, and environmental context to improve predictive accuracy, while investigating INRs to capture continuous-time signals and non-standard temporal resolutions.

---

### Research Interests

- 📈 **Contextual Forecasting:** Enhancing predictive models with high-dimensional context and metadata.
- 🧠 **Implicit Neural Representations (INRs):** Continuous coordinate-based neural networks for time series & signal modeling.
- ⚡ **Deep Learning for Signals:** Handling irregularly sampled, missing, and continuous temporal data.
